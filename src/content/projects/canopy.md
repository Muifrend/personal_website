Canopy was my first longer development project with a team. We built it across **Fall 2025 and Spring 2026** as Minerva University's Digital Balance civic project, with **Ezza Naveed as our civic partner**. My teammates were **Hina Shahnaz Javed, Kreeshna Mehta, and Michael Masłowski**.

Our research narrowed the problem from screen time to the gap between intention and action. Students need their laptops for work; the question is whether their activity matches what they planned. Canopy puts scheduled work beside observed computer activity in a calendar, with evidence users can inspect and supportive prompts when they drift.

I led much of the prototype implementation using **Electron, React, TypeScript, SQLite, and ActivityWatch**. The team contributed research, stakeholder communication, peer feedback, and presentation work.

We reached a working demo, a symposium presentation, and a [macOS beta](https://github.com/Muifrend/canopy/releases/tag/v1.0.0).  The [process writeup](https://github.com/Muifrend/canopy/blob/atom/docs/PROCESS.md) documents the journey in more detail.
