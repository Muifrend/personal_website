**Ghost PM** is an autonomous AI project manager built on OpenClaw that runs 24/7 across any messaging platform — silently turning conversations into structured Notion tasks without any manual input.

Shipped end-to-end in a 5-hour hackathon, Ghost PM addresses a real operational gap: project teams lose decisions, action items, and deadlines in the noise of async chat. Ghost PM listens continuously, detects what matters, and logs it — so nothing slips through.

**What I built:**
- **Agent Architecture** — Designed a context-aware message-to-task pipeline that reliably identifies actionable items from unstructured conversation across platforms.
- **Infrastructure & Deployment** — Self-hosted on a VPS with full remote setup, configuration, and iteration over SSH for true 24/7 uptime.
- **Notion Automation** — Built a live integration that captures tasks, owners, and context into a structured workspace in real time.
- **Full-Cycle Delivery** — Took Ghost PM from concept to working prototype to deployed system inside a single hackathon session.

Ghost PM isn't a demo — it's a running operations layer. It reflects how I build: find a real bottleneck, cut straight to the solution, and ship something that works before the day is over.
